Frequently asked questions

Coworking questions, answered.

What types of coworking plans do you offer?

Inspire Workplace offers hot desks, dedicated desks, private cabins, managed offices, virtual office plans and training room bookings.

Can I book a tour before signing up?

Yes. You can book a free tour to see the workspace, rooftop cafeteria, cabins and available seating before choosing a plan.

Do you provide a GST registration address?

Yes. The virtual office plan includes an ORR business address for GST registration, mail handling and meeting room hours.

Is Inspire Workplace suitable for teams?

Yes. Private cabins support 4 to 10 seats, while managed office plans can be customised for larger teams of around 15 to 50 seats.
